# Policy on Public Authority Requests for Personal Data

**Entity:** [Tagme legal entity name]
**Adopted:** July 6, 2026
**Owner:** [Founder/DPO name]
**Scope:** All personal data processed by the entity, including Platform Data received from Meta (Threads user IDs, usernames, post content, access tokens).

## 1. Legality review (required)

Any request from a public authority (police, security services, regulators, courts, or foreign authorities) for personal data must be forwarded to the Owner before any response. The Owner reviews whether the request: (a) is in writing and identifies its legal basis; (b) comes from an authority competent to make it; (c) is valid under the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan and, where relevant, the data-transfer terms of our service providers. No data is disclosed until this review is complete. Verbal or informal requests are refused and redirected to this written process.

## 2. Challenging unlawful requests

If the review concludes the request lacks a valid legal basis, is overbroad, or is otherwise unlawful, the entity will: seek narrowing or withdrawal from the requesting authority; and where necessary, challenge the request through available administrative or judicial channels before disclosing any data. External legal counsel is engaged for any contested request.

## 3. Data minimization

Where disclosure is legally required, the entity discloses only the minimum data necessary to satisfy the specific request — limited to the named individuals, data categories, and time period stated in it. Bulk or open-ended disclosure is not permitted. Access tokens and secrets are never disclosed.

## 4. Documentation

Every request is logged in a dedicated register recording: date received, requesting authority and named officials, stated legal basis, our legality assessment and reasoning, any challenge made and its outcome, what data (if any) was disclosed, and the date of the response. Records are retained for at least 5 years.

## 5. Notification

Where not prohibited by law, the entity will notify affected individuals of the request, and will notify Meta where required by the Meta Platform Terms.
